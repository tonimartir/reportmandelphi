Live demo

The sample reports of Report Manager, run by its JavaScript engine (@reportman on npm) in your browser: the parameters of each report, its pages and its PDF are made on this page, with nothing installed and no server. The data is the sample SQLite database. Try also the report designer, in your browser too.

    Made with the @reportman packages (MPL 2.0). The data is read with sql.js, SQLite compiled to WebAssembly, used only by this page: the packages are plain JavaScript. Fonts: Liberation, DejaVu and Noto (Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Thai, CJK), under their free licences.
