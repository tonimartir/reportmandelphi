Designer live demo

The web report designer of Report Manager (@reportman/designer on npm) in your browser: open a sample report, change its sections, items, expressions and SQL, preview its PDF and save it, or make a new report and let the AI copilot design it from a sentence, with one of the schemas of the sample database. Its server is this page itself: a service worker answers the designer with @reportman/designer-server and the JavaScript engine, on the sample SQLite database. What you save stays in this browser; what you ask the copilot goes to Reportman AI, with this browser's free credits or the account you log into in its chat. See also the reports run by the engine.

Made with the @reportman packages (MPL 2.0), Angular, Angular Material and Monaco (MIT; see their licences). The data is read with sql.js, SQLite compiled to WebAssembly, used only by this page.
