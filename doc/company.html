Report Manager was born in 1994 in Girona (Spain), near Barcelona, and has been providing its products and services for more than twenty years. The company that provides them today is REPORTMAN AI, S.L., incorporated in Girona in 2026 and led by Toni Martir, who wrote Report Manager and remains its CEO. It is the same company behind ai.reportman.es and erpaisoftware.com; its registered data are in the legal notice.
